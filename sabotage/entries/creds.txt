# Who a request authenticates as: the login, the URL's credentials and
# what they make (crates/curl-core/src/auth/creds.rs, options' Login).

=== an empty user or password the application gives is taken for none
file: crates/curl-core/src/options/mod.rs
witness: curl-core auth::creds::tests::a_part_given_empty_still_makes_credentials
--- old
            .all(|given| given.is_none())
--- new
            .all(|given| given.as_ref().is_none_or(Vec::is_empty))

=== a password the application gives alone is taken for none
file: crates/curl-core/src/options/mod.rs
witness: curl-core auth::creds::tests::a_part_given_empty_still_makes_credentials
--- old
        [user, password, bearer, service, authzid]
            .iter()
--- new
        [user, bearer, service, authzid]
            .iter()

=== credentials differing only in case are the same
file: crates/curl-core/src/auth/creds.rs
witness: curl-core auth::creds::tests::credentials_are_the_same_only_byte_for_byte
--- old
        same_secret(user, other.user)
            & same_secret(password, other.password)
--- new
        user.eq_ignore_ascii_case(other.user)
            & password.eq_ignore_ascii_case(other.password)

=== credentials with another token are the same
file: crates/curl-core/src/auth/creds.rs
witness: curl-core auth::creds::tests::credentials_are_the_same_only_byte_for_byte
--- old
            & same_secret(bearer, other.bearer)
--- new
            & (same_secret(bearer, other.bearer) | true)
